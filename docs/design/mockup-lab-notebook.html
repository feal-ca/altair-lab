Lab notebook, cooler: which paper?

Same structure and fonts as A. Only paper, grid, ink and accent change.

A

Engineering pad

Blue-grey paper and grid, navy-black ink, red pencil kept as the only warm note. Closest to the original A, just cooled down.

B

Graphite

Neutral grey paper, graphite ink, a brighter vermilion accent. No colour cast at all; the accent does all the talking.

C

Cyanotype

White paper with a cyan grid, deep-blue ink, orange accent. Reads like a blueprint or a science pad; the coolest of the three.
